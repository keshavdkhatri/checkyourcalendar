# Product Requirements

## Core Product Concept
"What is today, according to different calendars, and why does today matter?"

The system is built on a central content flow:
**DATE → CALENDAR → OBSERVANCE → MEANING → STORY → SOURCES**

### Bidirectional Navigation
Users should be able to navigate seamlessly through the ecosystem:
1. **Date-First:** Date → Calendars → Observances → Stories
2. **Event-First:** Festival → Story → Dates → Calendars

## Core Experiences

### Homepage "Today" Experience
- Today's Gregorian date prominently displayed.
- "Today" mapped across other supported calendar systems.
- Today's key observances and festivals.
- "Why today matters" (Brief summary).
- The story behind the day.
- "Explore more" (Links to deeper historical/cultural context).

### Progressive Disclosure
- **Simple first:** Clean, uncluttered UI showing the immediate answer ("Today is X").
- **Detail on demand:** Users can click to read the deep cultural story, exact calculations, or historical sources.

## User Personas & Stories
- **The Young Cultural Explorer (e.g., Young Gujarati User):** 
  *Example Use Case:* Asking "What is today according to the Gujarati calendar?" Should see not just a date, but meaning, context, and the story behind a specific fasting day or festival.
- **The Curious Global User:** Wants to know "What festival is today in Japan or India?" and understand the human element behind it.
- **The Student/Researcher:** Looking for accurate historical context and source-backed information for a school paper.

## Search Intent Support
The product must directly answer long-tail search queries:
- "What is today's Gujarati date?"
- "Why do people fast today?"
- "What festival is today?"
- "What is the story behind [festival]?"

## MVP Scope (RECOMMENDATION)
- **Calendars:** 3-5 calendar systems (e.g., Gregorian, Gujarati Vikram Samvat, Islamic Hijri, Hebrew, Julian).
- **Observances:** Core observances for the selected calendars.
- **Stories:** Detailed story pages for major upcoming observances.
- **Pages:** Homepage ("Today"), individual Date pages, Calendar overview pages, Festival/Story pages.

## Content Requirements
- **VERIFIED FACT:** Content must be researched, source-backed, culturally respectful, and clearly attributed.
- **NOT Requirements:** No mass AI-generated content (AI can assist in structuring, but facts/stories must be verified). No thin pages. No keyword stuffing. No copied content.

## Future Expansion
- More calendars (e.g., Chinese, Coptic, Persian).
- More localized observances.
- Deeper story integration and multimedia.
- Multiple languages.
- Regional variations of calendars.
